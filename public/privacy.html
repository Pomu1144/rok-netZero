Privacy Policy

Realm of Kings · Effective 6 October 2026

Summary

Realm of Kings does not collect, store or share any personal data. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data on your device

Your kingdom's progress is saved only on your own device (in the app's local storage). It never leaves the device unless you choose to copy a save code from Settings yourself.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example, when a building finishes). These are created on the device and are not sent through any server.

Network

The app runs fully offline. It makes no network requests while you play.

Children

Because no personal information is collected from anyone, none is collected from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
